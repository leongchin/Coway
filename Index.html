What makes Icon 2 different?

Most dispensers keep a tank of water hot all day. Icon 2 heats only what you pour, the moment you pour it, then filters, chills and measures it too. It is one compact unit doing the job of a kettle, a jug filter and fridge bottles.

What changes at home

No more waiting for the boil. No more fridge shelf lost to water bottles. No more guessing how much to pour for formula, tea or rice. Most families tell us the kettle goes into storage within a week. SAMPLE

How it works

Tap water flows through two filters, a Nano-Trap filter and a Plus Inno-Sense carbon filter, then is heated on demand or drawn from the cold tank, and poured in the amount you choose.

What it filters

Coway states its NanoTrap filter removes 98 types of harmful substances, including heavy metals such as lead and mercury, bacteria and viruses, chemical pollutants and environmental hormones.

Service, filters & warranty

Every plan includes 2 years of filters and a 1-year warranty. Choose Heart Service for technician visits, or Self-Service for filter kits delivered to your door.

How to use it

Tap the temperature, tap the volume, place your cup. That's it. Set your favourite amount once with My Capacity.

Who is Icon 2 for?

Busy households, new parents, tea and coffee lovers, and anyone in an HDB or condo who wants filtered hot and cold water without a bulky machine on the counter.

Questions, answered

Still have questions?

Will it fit my HDB kitchen?

Very likely. Icon 2 is 180mm wide, 340mm deep and 385mm tall. It needs a nearby power point and access to your water line, which our technician checks at installation.

Is the hot water really instant?

Yes. Icon 2 heats water as it pours instead of keeping a tank hot, so you get 45, 70, 85 or 98°C in seconds.

Heart Service or Self-Service: which should I pick?

Pick Heart Service if you'd rather never think about maintenance: a technician visits to check, sanitise and change filters. Pick Self-Service if you're happy to do a quick filter swap: kits arrive with an email reminder a week before.

Is it safe for baby formula?

The 45°C setting is designed for mixing formula. Always follow your formula's preparation instructions and your doctor's advice.

How long does installation take?

Usually under an hour. We call to book a slot that suits you. SAMPLE

What happens after 2 years?
